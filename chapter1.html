Monologue: New and Distant Things

The D-day of my life came true. I am entering into the world of corporate and very excited inside. I never stayed in any 5-star hotel ever... I don't even know what's it takes to keep me inside that.

The questions are going around my mind and I am trying to uphold that I am finally a job holder in a MNC company.

I took all my newly bought clothes with a hopes of finding something new in life.

I am not a great guy in love and I think I never actually embraced it correctly. Yet I am just loyal to it and I wanted to be righteous always.

Things are not so smooth for me lately when I joined the company but I hoped for better days to come… I just thought maybe it's just a tough time and I will survive it.

I am preparing myself to be a private person in the office, a guy who never tells anything to anyone but just do the work and leave.

Making friends felt like giving more space to new people and less to real ones. I have convinced myself to the maximum extent and I stepped in.

I have prepared myself that I just need to surpass the ITP and then I just live my life normally without anyone intervening in it. Because I have huge responsibility on me by being in a relationship and that's not going so great too which adds more responsibility to make it right and better.

Julie — An Interesting Looking City Girl

In midst of all these thoughts, I got a call from Kedar inviting me for the dinner. Silly me, I did not even check my clothes… I am wearing that Tathva T-shirt… I just don't care about anyone there anyway, so I just came in the same T-shirt of Tathva and shorts.

Then there is Kedar… then there is this sincere-looking gentleman who seems like a proper topper, who is none other than Shreyash Deshpandey… introduced himself as Shreyas. I noticed his chappal are the same as mine.

I also noticed a girl who wears a proper tucked-in formal outfit… a blue denim shirt and a black pant. She looks like some city girl and I thought definitely a rich person with such an accent and gestures.

I introduced myself and I heard “Julie”.

“Julie”… A name that fits more to cute puppies… that's what I thought and fixated my mind to think you are Christian from Hyderabad… maybe from a wealthy rich family.

I like to be casual with opposite gender to avoid any unnecessary stories that need to be conveyed to Nikki later at night. So I kept my distance for my peace of mind. I see Kedar been talking to you all the time and I just stayed by.

I also just thought you are a Kedar vibe. Kinda understood that he seems to be trying hard for your attention. I just let it slip and happy for him.

The first day has started and I am getting ready for the office… took a picture to send to Nikki. She said I am looking good and fit… and she told me she is upset that I could just be friends with someone in the office and leave her aside.

I tried to tell her it's not the case… then conversation went other way around with she being mad at me… I got sad. I just kept things aside and started to office anyway.

Julie to Juhi — An Interesting and Curious Fact

I saw you and Kedar sitting next to each other and I took my opportunity to sit with Ramanan. I just get to know that your name is Juhi when I saw the attendance sheet… also I saw your surname is Agastyaraju… I know it's Brahmin surname and I kinda don't get it. Coz you definitely don't seem like one.

Either way I just shut my brain and continued listening.

Again we are all back to the rooms. I just called my mom and I told her about your name… I told her I kinda liked how it sounds and it's short, stylish at the same time. Also asking her whether Agastyaraju is a Brahmin name or not.

She kinda confirmed and she was saying to me that she liked that name actually. Mom being mom… happy about me getting Brahmin friends finally :).

The same day we all met near PSN and that's the first time I kinda spoke to you. I know Kedar is boasting too much for you regarding what we do at college, which I really feel overrated as I know myself and college to the full extent.

Yet I like how you respectfully react to him without thinking that he is just bullshitting. I just thought I will give some reality checks from my side. I told you we are just loud but no work, especially when you are thinking that we did a lot in college…

I just liked that small convo with you… also how you appreciate the work of other without comparing yourself with it.

Eventually an unknown guilt and thought came up… I just thought if I get an opportunity to tell I am in a relationship… I would like to use it to make sure you can be true and free with me. For some reason I got the chance and I told so… then I am happy.

Then I message Nikki… I told her about you and Kedar… how he speaks without stopping… then Sarika called. We have a convo about you and Shreyash.

Yeah, then I just got curious about you and I want to be a friend with you. Yet you being Brahmin, me being in relationship sounded a bad move if I become a friend to you, especially to deal with the things.

I told myself to be like a stranger to maximum extent unless I couldn't resist to be friends with you.

Helping and Exploring

It's the second day of the office and we are on our way for breakfast. Then go for the office eventually.

Lately, I am a bit shy to order food. In fact I am indecisive to take anything or even the dosa. I kinda hoped someone can help me without judging me. I could ask Ramanan but somehow I hesitated.

You felt like a nice person who could lend me a hand and you speak Telugu. So while walking towards office, I just asked you with slight shaky voice:

“Hey, can you help me with ordering dosa?”

I feel so embarrassed right next minute. Thoughts are going around and I keep questioning myself, “Did she think that I am just creating an unnecessary seek of attention from her or what does she think about it?” (What did you really think?)

You replied with your sweet voice: “Yeah, of course, I will order it next time when I bring it for myself.”

For a moment… I am relieved so much. I do feel free and happy that you reacted nicely to it.

It's the third day we went for the dinner. Lately you started calling me for ordering a dosa for me. I am so glad that you gave such priority to my words. I couldn't keep it to myself and I told Nikki about it without thinking twice.

Being Nikki, I understand it's not her mistake to feel so much jealous when a guy overshares about a girl who he just met. And it's a classic move for a guy to get in touch with a girl.

Genuinely I felt a little shy and I asked for it, but I couldn't help thinking of it any other way.

I know you a little and I wanted to know more about you, yet you felt like a very private person. It's just the second day—what do I expect though…

Laundry and Night Walks

Third day when we are back from the office, you have asked me about the laundry stuff. Me being me, I have already ordered the laundry and then added your slot too. I am just a lazy guy and happy that someone else is able to join me.

That's the first time it's just two of us, especially without Kedar going bak bak around you. I just had a genuine convo with you and it's nice to have a chat with you (💕 still though).

The sunset, the steps and simple talks felt so light, like a slight breeze taking a leaf of burden.

I have asked the most embarrassed question:

“Can I see your view?”

You paused for a minute and I thought of going back in time… and not asking it for a second.

You just told something and you left, so… I just went to my room cursing my loose tongue and bad timing. I told Sarika in between by calling her, just before dinner, what I have asked and what actually happened between us.

Later, when you informed me that your dad is around, I am so relieved and I find it better than thinking me as a pervert trying to sneak into your room.

Later someday it is planned to go out for the dinner with Kedar and co. For some reason, I got late. I think I had a quarrel with Nikki and I am sad, so I told them I just changed my mind.

At the same time you have dropped out too. I felt nice to have your company and that's the first time we had a conversation and you told me about Ajay.

I find the conversation nice, but the story got to me as if Ajay is just a friend who is looking out for an opportunity to become a boyfriend.

Thoughts around Ajay being a manipulator, pulling you into it without your consent, and you just followed him eventually, started liking him and falling for him in the process.

Yet you miss him too in your sentences and I don't really get who is wrong or who is right. But I understand that there is something between you two, stronger than friendship.

I really didn't think of it as a serious relationship but more of a friends-turn-into-lovers situation in a college environment.

I am confused and at the same time also admired the story, especially when you talked about compatibility and how you have told him what it's like for you being in a marriage.

I never had such clarity about marriage, in fact love too. All I know is I want to have a person to love and I want to be loved. Never thought beyond it and above it.

Adjusting is my baseline for a relationship and having a settled life is the goal of the marriage.

But for some reason, I felt so much enlightened listening to you… also starting questioning myself.

For an instance, it just felt like a void when I really asked myself about my relationship. I felt like a coward before you.

And Nikki is such a nice person, always kept me before her many times.

Yet all I thought was how to make it work, how do I achieve what I want, but never asked myself what is it like to be in a relationship really? Do I like my relationship? Am I really happy?

Am I the good one?

Every thought changed for a bit, but I thought that it's good I met a very matured friend who really can help me in my emotional side.
